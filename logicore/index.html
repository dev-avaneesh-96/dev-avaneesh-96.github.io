01 · The idea

Cold-chain goods are fragile.
The paperwork around them shouldn't be.

Medicines, vaccines, dairy and fresh produce must stay inside a temperature range from the factory shelf to the customer's door. Today that journey is managed with phone calls, paper slips and spreadsheets — so when a box goes missing or a truck runs warm, nobody knows where or when it happened.

LogiCore replaces that with a single system: every box is scanned at every hand-off, every truck is tracked, and every step is recorded.

From manual coordination to a recorded, traceable journey

What we are building, in one picture

Know what you hold

Warehouses with scanned boxes and pallets, so stock and location are always exact.

Know where it is

Trucks and drivers tracked live on a map, with every trip and stop recorded.

Know it stayed cold

Temperature measured inside the truck for the whole journey, with alerts when it drifts.

The core promise: a complete, tamper-evident record of every box — who handled it, which truck carried it, where it was, and what temperature it travelled at.

02 · The platform

One foundation, switchable systems

LogiCore is built as a shared core with separate systems on top. Each customer organisation gets only the systems it needs; they switch on or off per organisation and work together automatically when combined.

LogiCore architecture — apps on top, switchable systems in the middle, shared core underneath

Why this shape matters

  • Start small, grow naturally. A customer can begin with trucks only, add warehouses later — no migration.
  • Systems cooperate. With WMS and TMS both on, a warehouse dispatch becomes a truck trip automatically.
  • Every organisation is isolated. One customer can never see another's data.
  • One login, one record. People, locations and boxes are defined once and shared by every system.

04 · WMS — Warehouse

Every box accounted for, from dock to shelf

Live

The warehouse system tracks goods as physical units — individual boxes grouped onto pallets — using barcode scans rather than manual entry. Workers scan; the system does the book-keeping.

Inside the warehouse — the lifecycle of a box

What it does

  • Seven scan activities on the mobile app: Inbound, Outbound, Box Putaway, Convert to Pallet, Pallet Putaway, Pallet Consolidation, Confirm & Stage.
  • Format checks on every scan — a wrong or unknown barcode is caught immediately, with an accept/decline choice.
  • Resumable sessions — if a worker's phone dies mid-task, they pick up exactly where they left off.
  • Duplicate protection — scanning the same box twice is flagged instantly.

Teams & control

  • Several managers per warehouse, each with their own team of workers.
  • Box enquiry — scan any box to see its full journey and current status.
  • Missing or mismatched boxes are raised as exceptions for a manager to resolve, not silently lost.
  • Warehouses defined with standard state / district / pincode data for clean reporting.

05 · TMS — Transport

Every truck visible, every trip recorded

Live

The transport system manages the fleet, the drivers, and the trips between locations — and watches the vehicles in real time. No driver needs to press "start" or "stop": tracking is automatic.

Live route view — a truck's journey drawn from its GPS history (illustrative)

Fleet & drivers

Trucks and drivers belong to the organisation. A truck can only be assigned drivers and trips from its own company.

Trips & stops

A trip has a truck, a driver and one stop per destination. Status moves Planned → Loading → In transit → Completed.

Automatic arrival

Each location has a virtual boundary. When the truck leaves or arrives, the system records it — no manual check-in.

Always-on tracking from a truck-mounted phone

Instead of expensive custom hardware, a company phone is enrolled to one truck by an administrator and stays mounted there. It keeps recording when the driver changes, when the app is closed, and when there is no signal.

  • Moving → position every ~15 seconds. Parked → every ~2 minutes to save battery.
  • Idle time and stops are detected automatically and shown in the trip summary.
  • Works offline — everything is stored on the phone first and uploaded when coverage returns.

07 · Cold-chain monitoring

Proof that it stayed cold

Location live Temperature next

Each truck carries a phone that reports position today, and will also read a Bluetooth temperature logger placed in the load. Readings are captured from the start of loading to the end of unloading.

From the truck to the control room — data is never lost if the signal drops

Two temperature classes

Chosen per shipment at booking; one range per truck trip, so classes are never mixed.

Illustrative temperature trace

Time out of range, and how long, is recorded for each trip.

Who gets told: if temperature leaves its range, boxes go missing, or an express deadline is at risk, the alert reaches the driver, the sender, the receiver and the hub manager together.

08 · CNS — Courier Network

A cold-chain courier, hub to hub

Next · pilot targeted Dec 2026

The next system turns the platform into a full courier network. A courier company runs central hubs and trucks; many senders hand over boxes and many receivers get them — all on one tracked, temperature-controlled chain.

Hub-and-spoke courier network — many senders, two hubs, many receivers
  • Always via two hubs — consistent handling and one control point at each end.
  • Every hand-off scanned — seller → pickup truck → hub → line-haul → hub → delivery truck → receiver.
  • Verified parties — each seller and buyer is verified in person before they can transact.
  • Express & Normal service, with deadlines and priority loading for express.
  • Receivers install nothing — the happy code arrives by SMS.
  • Pilot first: hub-to-hub movement goes live first; pickup and delivery follow.

09 · Trust & reliability

Designed to be trusted with real goods

Logistics software runs in warehouses with weak signal and with people who have little time. LogiCore is built around that reality.

Privacy

Each customer is walled off

Every record belongs to one organisation. Rules enforced on the server — not just hidden in the screen — stop one customer ever seeing another's trucks, stock or people.

Control

Sensitive actions go through the server

Creating users, moving stock or changing a trip's status is validated centrally, so a modified app cannot bypass the rules.

Traceability

Nothing is overwritten

Every scan, status change and approval is appended to a permanent history with who, when and where. Users are suspended, never deleted.

Resilience

Works without signal

Scans and GPS are stored on the device first and replayed safely later — the same scan can never be counted twice.
